The \eTwoNScale-repeat pool sweep in Section~\ref{sec:perf-read} is the campaign on the same \(10{,}000\)-frame file.
Paired one-thread/two-thread Python speedups have median \(\eTwoPyTwoMed\) with BCa interval \([\eTwoPyTwoLo, \eTwoPyTwoHi]\);
four threads give \(\eTwoPyFourMed\) with \([\eTwoPyFourLo, \eTwoPyFourHi]\);
eight threads give \(\eTwoPyEightMed\) with \([\eTwoPyEightLo, \eTwoPyEightHi]\).
Those three intervals are the \texttt{python\_full} entries of \texttt{e2\_speedup\_bca.json} (\texttt{pixi run cpc-e2-speedup-bca}).
The in-process file read is \(\eTwoFileRead\,\mathrm{s}\) and the boundary scan is \(\eTwoScan\,\mathrm{s}\).
Their sum is \(\eTwoFloorSum\,\mathrm{s}\), which is \(\eTwoFloorFrac\) of the 15-repeat one-thread median \(\eTwoRustOneNative\,\mathrm{s}\).
That quotient is not the serial fraction of Figure~\ref{fig:roofline}.
The figure has no Amdahl curve.
