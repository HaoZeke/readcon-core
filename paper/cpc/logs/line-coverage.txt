Physical line coverage of the library sources.

A line is executable when a counted coverage region covers it, and hit
when any same-source execution has a count above zero. llvm-cov report
is not the count used here. Nothing is excluded.

Two instrumented builds, source-identical, scored apart and then as an
explicit union. Lean features are python, zstd, and parallel. The
second build adds chemfiles-from-sources. Profiles from the two builds
are not merged as raw samples. Each build has its own profdata. The
union takes the higher count on each line.

hit/total is lines with a count above zero over executable lines.
